Delete Your RenewBook Account

Last updated: 08/06/2026

You can request deletion of your RenewBook account and all associated data at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

To request deletion: email us from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Account deletion request."

Email ajmidjourney@gmail.com

Deleting individual data, without deleting your account

You don't need to delete your entire account to remove specific data. Within the RenewBook app, you can delete any individual subscription entry at any time:

  1. Open the subscription you want to remove from your Dashboard
  2. Tap "Delete subscription"
  3. Confirm the deletion

What's deleted: that subscription's name, price, category, currency, billing cycle, renewal date, and any scheduled renewal reminders for it.

What's kept: your account (email, login credentials) and all other subscriptions you haven't chosen to delete.

Retention: deletion is immediate -- deleted subscription data is not retained afterward.

Deleting your entire account

Once we receive and verify your request, we will fully delete:

  • Your account (email and authentication credentials)
  • All subscription data you entered (names, prices, categories, renewal dates)
  • Any preferences or settings associated with your account

This is a full deletion, not a deactivation -- your data is not retained in an inactive state.

How long it takes

We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days of receiving and verifying your request.

Alternative: delete directly in the app

If you still have RenewBook installed, you can also start this same request from within the app: Settings > Account Actions > Delete account.

Questions

Privacy Policy for Ledger

Last updated: [FILL IN DATE BEFORE PUBLISHING]

Ledger ("we," "our," "the app") is a subscription-tracking app. This policy explains what information Ledger collects, why, and how you can control it.

Ledger does not sell your data, does not show ads, and does not share your data with advertisers. The only outside parties involved are the infrastructure providers described below, who process data solely to make the app work.

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password (we never see or store your password in plain text). If you sign in with Google, GitHub, or Apple instead, we receive the email address associated with that account from the provider.

Subscription data you enter. The names, categories, prices, currencies, billing cycles, and renewal dates of the subscriptions you choose to track. This is the core data the app is built around — it never leaves your account and is never used for anything other than showing it back to you.

Biometric data. If you enable fingerprint/face unlock, that authentication happens entirely on your device using your operating system's built-in biometric APIs. Ledger never receives, stores, or transmits your fingerprint or face data — your device handles this itself and only tells our app "unlocked" or "not unlocked."

Notification scheduling. To remind you before a renewal, Ledger schedules local notifications on your device. This currently happens entirely on-device; we do not operate a remote push notification server at this time.

Purchase information (Pro subscribers only). If you purchase Ledger Pro, the purchase is processed by Google Play Billing (and Apple's equivalent on iOS). We receive confirmation that you have an active subscription, but we do not receive or store your payment card details — those are handled entirely by Google/Apple.

How we use this information

  • To create and secure your account
  • To display your subscriptions, spending totals, and renewal reminders back to you
  • To detect potentially overlapping subscriptions within your own account (this happens on your data only, never compared against other users)
  • To provide customer support if you contact us
  • To determine whether your account has an active Pro subscription

Who we share data with

We do not sell or rent your data to anyone. We use the following service providers to operate the app, each of whom processes data only as needed to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider, which stores your account and subscription data securely.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple App Store — process Pro subscription payments (Pro subscribers only). We do not receive your payment details.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Ledger or our users.

Data security

Your data is protected with row-level security, meaning your subscription data is only ever accessible to your own account — not to other users, and not by default to us either. Data is encrypted in transit between your device and our servers.

Data retention and deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at [SUPPORT_EMAIL]. Once we receive your request, we will fully delete your account and all associated subscription data — not just deactivate it.

Children's privacy

Ledger is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. You can exercise any of these by contacting us at [SUPPORT_EMAIL]. Most of this — viewing, editing, or deleting your subscription data — you can also do directly in the app at any time.

International users

Ledger is available to users in multiple countries and currencies. Your data may be processed in a country other than the one you live in, by our infrastructure providers listed above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "last updated" date above and, where required, notify you in the app.
